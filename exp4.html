Our story

A flower shop that still cuts to order.

Petal & Stem started as a single roadside stall and a wheelbarrow of buckets. We still visit the growers ourselves, still cut each stem the morning it ships, and still turn away anything that won't open properly — because one honest bouquet beats a shelf of tired ones.

Everything is arranged by hand after your order comes in, so what you receive was still growing a day earlier.
